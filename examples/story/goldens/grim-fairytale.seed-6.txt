Kat, the ranger, walked into the mountain inn after riding three nights without sleep. Pouria, the liar, watched beside the fire.
When Kat asked which road crossed the pass, Pouria said that the bell-road was safe. He hid a smile when she thanked him.
Before dawn, Kat followed his directions because the snow had covered every other trail. At the first bell, the trees shut out the moon.
Kat found six frozen travelers beneath the bell tower, each holding a coin from the inn. She turned back, though the bells rang without wind behind her.
At sunrise, Kat returned and laid the six coins before Pouria. He called them worthless, until the dead travelers knocked at the door.
Kat opened it. The dead led Pouria up the bell-road, and by nightfall his voice was ringing from the tower.
