Maria, the knight, limped into the mountain inn after riding three nights without sleep. Pouria, the swindler, sat beside the fire.
When Maria asked which road crossed the pass, Pouria said that the bell-road was safe. He hid a smile when she thanked him.
Before dawn, Maria followed his directions because the snow had covered every other trail. At the first bell, the trees began to whisper.
Maria found six frozen travelers beneath the bell tower, each holding a coin from the inn. She turned back, though the bells rang without wind behind her.
At sunrise, Maria returned and laid the six coins before Pouria. He denied knowing them, until the dead travelers knocked at the door.
Maria opened it. The dead dragged Pouria up the bell-road, and by nightfall his voice was ringing from the tower.
